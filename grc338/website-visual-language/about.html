Sweet Bean Coffee was founded on a simple belief: a truly exceptional cup of coffee should tell a story. From bright, fruit-forward single origins to balanced, carefully developed roasts, we aim to highlight the distinct character of every bean we serve.

Built on craft, intention, and hospitality, Sweet Bean Coffee is dedicated to creating a focused and elevated coffee experience. Every detail matters — from sourcing and preparation to the way each drink is served.

While coffee is at the center of what we do, we’ve chosen to offer one food item and do it exceptionally well: our homemade specialty chocolate chip cookie. Thoughtfully crafted to pair perfectly with our drinks, it’s a simple indulgence made with care.

More than a café, Sweet Bean Coffee is a space rooted in quality and community — where attention behind the bar translates into excellence in every cup
